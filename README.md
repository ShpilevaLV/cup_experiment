# Experiment Report: VLM-enhanced Urban Planning with LLM Agents

**Reference**
Ni H. Planning, Living and Judging: A Multi-agent LLM-based Framework for Cyclical Urban Planning / Hang Ni, Yuzhi Wang, Hao Liu. // arXiv.org. – URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.20505.

## Goal
Test whether adding visual observations (simulated VLM) to LLM prompts improves the quality of generated urban zoning plans.

## Method
- 3 real Moscow brownfield sites: ZIL Severny, Serp i Molot, Nagatinsky Zaton.
- Each site received two prompts: Baseline (text-only) and Enhanced (text + VLM observations).
- Plans were generated using GPT-4o (ChatGPT).
- Automatic faithfulness evaluation checked: area accuracy, green space percentage, building height, flood risk compliance.
- Expert evaluation rated realism, safety, and justification quality (scale 0–3 per site).

## Results

### Faithfulness (automatic)
| Site | Baseline | Enhanced | Winner |
|------|----------|----------|--------|
| ZIL Severny | 0.700 | 0.727 | Enhanced |
| Serp i Molot | 1.000 | 0.875 | Baseline |
| Nagatinsky Zaton | 0.889 | 0.889 | Tie |

### Expert Evaluation (human)
| Site | Baseline score | Enhanced score | Winner |
|------|----------------|----------------|--------|
| ZIL Severny | 0 | 3 | Enhanced |
| Serp i Molot | 2 | 1 | Baseline |
| Nagatinsky Zaton | 0.5 | 2.5 | Enhanced |

### Combined Results
| Site | Faithfulness Winner | Expert Winner | Overall Winner |
|------|---------------------|---------------|----------------|
| ZIL Severny | Enhanced | Enhanced | Enhanced |
| Serp i Molot | Baseline | Baseline | Baseline |
| Nagatinsky Zaton | Tie | Enhanced | Enhanced |

Enhanced approach won in 2 out of 3 sites, demonstrating that VLM observations tend to improve plan quality, especially for large and complex territories.

## Discussion
- For ZIL (146.74 ha), VLM observations helped better account for shoreline and noise, resulting in higher expert scores despite similar faithfulness.
- Serp i Molot (4.54 ha) showed that on small, highly constrained sites, extra visual context may overload the model, causing minor metric drops.
- Nagatinsky Zaton (29 ha) benefited from VLM in expert evaluation, particularly in amenity placement and pedestrian connectivity.

## Limitations
- VLM observations were simulated manually, not generated by a real vision-language model.
- Only three sites were tested; a larger sample is needed for statistical significance.
- A single LLM (GPT-4o) was used; results may vary with other models.

## Conclusion
The experiment partially confirms the hypothesis: adding visual context improves urban planning generation in most cases. Future work will involve a real VLM pipeline and testing on more diverse sites.
